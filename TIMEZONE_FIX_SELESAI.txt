====================================================
  PENJELASAN UTC vs WIB
====================================================

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time):
- Greenwich Mean Time
- Reference timezone dunia
- Offset: +0

WIB (Waktu Indonesia Barat):
- UTC + 7 jam
- Contoh:
  UTC 06:18 → WIB 13:18 ✅
  UTC 00:00 → WIB 07:00
  UTC 17:00 → WIB 00:00 (next day)
