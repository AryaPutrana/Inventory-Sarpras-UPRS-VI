  FORMAT WAKTU
====================================================

REALTIME (now()):
- Date: 2026-09-29 (hari ini)
- Time: 13:12 (jam sekarang)
- Timezone: Asia/Jakarta (dari config/app.php)

HTML5 FORMAT:
- Pattern: Y-m-d\TH:i
- Example: 2026-09-29T13:12
- T = Separator between date & time

DATABASE FORMAT (auto convert):
- Saved as: 2026-09-29 13:12:00
- Column: taken_at (datetime)
